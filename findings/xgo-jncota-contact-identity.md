### Jncota factory company

```text
深圳市晶科泰科技有限公司
```

```text
landline: 0755-27929877

```

## Interpretation

Evidence level: **CONFIRMED organizational/contact identity**.

This establishes that XGO brand operations and Jncota shared both the same landline and the same mobile sales/contact number, independently strengthening the already documented XGO/Jncota corporate lineage.

It does **not** by itself prove that Jncota physically assembled the exact A10 PCB, designed its firmware, or supplied 1688 offer `754381935521`.

Exact-A10 manufacturing attribution still requires at least one direct bridge such as:

- an A10 factory listing under Jncota/XGO with exact enclosure;
- exact A10 PCB/manufacturing markings;
- stock-card/firmware provenance;
- offer/image-owner account tied to the company;
- factory or certification document naming A10.

## Related supply-chain caution

A separate current 1688 wholesaler, `深圳市墨咔科技有限公司` (`szmkkj.1688.com`), also uses landline `0755-27929877` but a different mobile number. It currently sells game hardware including DY09-type game/power-bank products.

This remains a **PARTIAL supply-chain clue**, not proof that 墨咔 supplied the A10. Searches have not tied it directly to A10, offer `754381935521`, or the known exact Alibaba image-owner namespaces.
