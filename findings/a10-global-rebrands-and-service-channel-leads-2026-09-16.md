## Regional service lead — Vietnam

`Game Tâm An` in Vietnam currently indexes the XGO A10 specifically as:

`Sạc dự phòng kiêm máy chơi game XGO A10 – 10.000mAh – 22.5W`

and describes FC/NES, SFC/SNES, GBA, GBC, GB, Megadrive and arcade support including IGS/CPS1/CPS2/NEOGEO.

More importantly for archaeology, the same shop explicitly sells replacement parts/accessories for handhelds and advertises customer support and a 7-day defective-goods exchange process. It is therefore a potentially useful regional service-channel lead: shops handling defective A10 units are more likely than ordinary reviewers to have seen the internals or retained original TF-card data.

Source: https://gametaman.com/

Do not infer that the shop repairs A10 boards unless independently confirmed.

## Negative firmware search result

Searches combining the newly recovered reseller aliases with `firmware`, repair/disassembly vocabulary, and known XGO/SF2000-family fingerprints (`bisrv.asd`, `Archive.sys`, `KeyMapInfo.kmp`, WQW) did not expose an independent public firmware repository or teardown in this pass.

The known SBCGaming A10 firmware thread remains the only clearly indexed user-level discussion located so far that explicitly discusses the missing stock card and SF2000-family relationship. Its RK3566 claim remains disputed and should not be adopted.
