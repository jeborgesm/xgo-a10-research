# DY19 external recovery ecosystem

## 2026-09-07 findings

### Independent Chinese firmware/recovery repost

A second, independent forum preserves the DY-19 recovery package trail:

- Site: 肖琪模拟游戏站 (xqemu.cn)
- Thread: `DY-19充电宝掌机救砖固件及软件分享`
- Posted: 2024-10-18
- Author shown by indexed page: `lengyufeiyan`
- Bilibili source: `BV1Td8ceJEA8`, uploader `Sesn`, published 2024-07-19
- Tianyi Cloud package: `https://cloud.189.cn/t/7V3mu2MJnUBf`
- Access code: `9fma`
- Bilibili text also names `炒鸡大帅比9961` as another uploader who produced an optimized firmware package.

This matters because the DY19 material was not confined to 4PDA. There is a Chinese owner/modding/recovery ecosystem with independently reposted resources. That gives us additional identities, domains and artifact URLs to search for teardown/board photographs.

### Handhelds Wiki

The DY19 has a dedicated Handhelds Wiki entry. Indexed specifications:
- 3.2-inch 320x240 display
- Linux
- 142x95x28 mm
- listed 6000 mAh battery
- stock firmware link
- SF2000 Multicore compatibility
- multiple additional images

The wiki's battery claim conflicts with XENON's physical teardown observation (~4000 mAh), making the teardown particularly valuable as physical evidence rather than reseller specification.

### Retail/replacement-parts trail

A Shopee listing indexed in 2026 explicitly advertises `DY19配件` (DY19 accessories) and contains 12 product images. Replacement-parts listings are a new search vector because sellers may expose bare shells, displays, boards, button membranes, or internal assemblies even when ordinary retail listings show only the assembled device.

### Search image sweep

Direct Chinese image searches for `DY-19充电宝掌机 拆机`, `DY19 主板 游戏机`, and English teardown variants did not yet surface a verified DY19 internal photograph. One visually similar controller/power-bank teardown surfaced but is a different BY-X8 device and must not be treated as DY19 evidence.
