# A10 archaeology — Game Power Bank search pivot

Date: 2026-09-16

## Search strategy

Pivot away from model-first searching and prioritize the generic/commercial identity used on the product itself and across reseller channels:

- `10000mAh game power bank`
- `Game Power Bank`
- `Games Power`
- `Power Bank Game Console 2 in 1`
- Chinese equivalents such as `10000mAh 游戏机充电宝`, `游戏机移动电源`, `游戏机充电宝`
- then combine the physical fingerprint: 22.5W wired, 15W wireless, 37Wh, 5800mAh rated, 141×67×20mm, six face buttons, joystick, AV/TF/USB-C.

## Strong new commercial identity: MechZone A10

Searches using the generic Game Power Bank terminology exposed a western/direct-import identity that matches the physical A10 fingerprint extremely closely: **MechZone A10**.

Independent indexed sources describe the MechZone A10 as:

- model A10;
- 10000mAh / 37Wh;
- rated capacity 5800mAh;
- 22.5W wired charging;
- 15W wireless charging;
- dimensions 67×141×20mm;
- 10 emulators / 10,000+ games;
- joystick plus six face buttons;
- external video output.

Notebookcheck published a MechZone A10 article on 2024-08-31 sourced from Banggood. Historical Banggood coupon/index mirrors show the product was actively sold under the MechZone A10 identity in 2024–2025.

This is materially stronger than a generic A10 naming collision because the **entire physical/electrical fingerprint matches**, not just the model number.

## Historical family precedent from generic terminology

The same generic `10000mAh 游戏机移动电源` wording retrieves a 2020 Charging Head teardown of an older `3th game / GAME BOX POWER` device. It is not the A10, but confirms that generic product-category terminology is highly productive for teardown archaeology. That older device used separate power/game PCB areas, a bonded game processor, Samsung K5L2731CAA-D770 MCP, and LM4890S 1W audio amp.

Source: `https://www.chongdiantou.com/archives/48095.html`

Do not project those components onto A10 without direct evidence.

## New investigation targets

1. Treat **MechZone A10** as a first-class alias for the exact specimen and search reviews, comments, support requests, firmware dumps, broken-unit sales, teardown photos, and cached Banggood media.
2. Recover historical Banggood product media and customer review images; review images are especially promising for broken/open units.
3. Search the exact generic fingerprint without brands: `Power Bank Game Console 2 in 1`, `10000mAh game power bank`, 37Wh, 5800mAh, 141×67×20mm, 22.5W + 15W.
4. Mine the SWPL GAMING XGO A10 video and its comments/related-video graph for users asking about firmware, SD cards, repairs or opening the unit.
5. Search Chinese second-hand/repair vocabulary around `10000mAh 游戏机充电宝` rather than only XGO/A10/DY10.
6. Continue FCC-document recovery separately; do not conflate the FCC A10 until enclosure/PCB imagery matches.

## Confidence update

**High confidence:** `MechZone A10` is a commercial/rebrand identity for the same A10 product design or an extremely close white-label instance, because model, dimensions, battery/rated capacity, wired/wireless charging specifications and control layout all align.

**Not yet proven:** MechZone's supply-chain relationship to XGO/芯果/Jinhangzhaobang, exact PCB revision identity, or whether all MechZone A10 units carry the same firmware/hardware revision as the specimen under investigation.
