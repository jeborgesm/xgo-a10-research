## High-confidence identity fingerprint

The following combination remains the strongest discriminator for the physical XGO A10 family:

- transparent horizontal game-console/power-bank enclosure
- approximately 141 × 67 × 20 mm
- 10000mAh nominal / 5800mAh rated / 37Wh
- 22.5W-class wired charging marketing; manual also contains 20W figures
- 15W magnetic wireless charging
- joystick + Select/Start + six face buttons
- TF/microSD, USB-C, AV/video/controller support
- ten-emulator marketing / 10,000-game marketing
- A10 on known XGO specimens

## Search result assessment

The generic-name search continues to reveal many regional/rebrand listings that omit XGO entirely. This reinforces that XGO/A10-only searches have poor recall.

Known aliases/distribution identities accumulated so far include XGO/芯果 A10, MechZone A10, SMARTBERRY A10, Pogopirate A10, Royaldraid A10, and fully generic `Power Bank Game Console 2 in 1` listings. These should be treated as commercial aliases/rebrands unless independent evidence establishes a manufacturer relationship.

Regional service-channel searches are now a priority because a seller or repairer handling defective units is more likely than a reviewer to expose PCB photography, TF-card contents, board markings, or replacement-part information.

## FCC 2AUZ9-A10 remains an identity gate

FCC ID `2AUZ9-A10` remains unresolved. Exact released exhibit identifiers previously recovered:

- external photographs: 7989625
- internal photographs: 7989626
- user manual: 7989627

The filing describes a magnetic wireless charging power bank and uses model A10. The applicant is East Sky Industry Co., Limited. Neither the model string nor product category is sufficient to identify it as the XGO Games Power device.

Do not merge this FCC filing into the XGO lineage until the released external/internal photographs can be retrieved and visually matched to the known enclosure/PCB geometry.

## Negative evidence

Expanded combinations of generic product names, aliases, `bisrv.asd`, `Archive.sys`, `KeyMapInfo.kmp`, WQW, firmware, teardown, repair and motherboard vocabulary have still not produced an independently indexed XGO A10 reverse-engineering repository or exact PCB teardown.

This is useful negative evidence, not proof that no prior work exists. Discord/Telegram/QQ/private communities, deleted marketplace reviews, repair channels, and unindexed seller media remain plausible locations.
