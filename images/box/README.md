# XGO A10 Retail Box — Primary-Source Photos

This directory is reserved for photographs of the physical retail packaging supplied with the newly purchased XGO / XGO PLUS+ A10 unit.

## Why preserve the box

The packaging is useful primary-source provenance evidence because it connects several product identity and feature layers visible on the physical item:

- `XGO` / `XGO PLUS+` consumer branding
- `GAME POWER BANK` / `Game console power bank` product identity
- `10000mAh` battery marketing
- `20W` / fast-charge marketing
- magnetic wireless charging
- emulator-family claims

One side of the photographed box explicitly advertises `Top 10 emulators` and visibly lists `ARCADE, FC, GB, GBA, GBC, MD, SFC...`. This is useful corroborating evidence for the stock firmware's console-family presentation, but the packaging claim should not by itself be treated as proof of the exact underlying emulator binaries.

The retail box does not prominently use `A10` as the consumer-facing name. The included manual and physical device labeling provide the stronger A10 model evidence. Preserve those as separate but related evidence sets rather than treating every identifier as interchangeable.

## Intended contents

Store original box photographs here, including:

- front/main panel
- side panels
- emulator/platform-list panel
- charging/power-bank feature panels
- opened-box presentation / tray when useful

Device-only photographs, manual pages, shipping/manufacturer labels, and teardown photographs should remain in their own evidence areas rather than being mixed into this directory.

## Privacy

Retail-box photographs are suitable for the public research archive only after checking that no shipping label, customer address, tracking number, or other personal information is visible in the frame. Do not copy unredacted shipping-label photographs into this directory.

## Evidence status

Source: photographs taken from the physical packaging received with the project's newly purchased AliExpress XGO-like/XGO PLUS+ unit.

Classification: **primary-source physical evidence**.
